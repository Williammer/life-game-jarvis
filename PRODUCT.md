# Jarvis prototype lab

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Throwaway interactive HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype, following the user's acceptance of the proposed prototype scope. Serve with Python stdlib. Production architecture remains BuilderIO agent-native plus selected Apace frontend foundations; this prototype does not claim framework integration.

## Users

One person using desktop and mobile web to decide and complete important daily actions. Chinese is the primary interface language.

## Product Purpose

Turn morning constraints and aspirations into user-confirmed game quests, then record evidence and observe scores and real module growth. Health and AI learning/practice lead; parenting stays lightweight.

## Positioning

Strategy over limited time and energy, RPG progression, quest lines, real societal exemplars and measurable gaps. Gamification is the decision and motivation mechanism, not a decorative overlay.

## Operating Context

Approximately three minutes of morning dialogue, quantitative comparison and confirmation. Completion and evidence can be entered at any later time. Rest, interrupted days and partial completion matter. Goals and exemplar candidates are proposed from context, then confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

User explicitly requested 3–5 aligned prototypes for EACH of five stages. This lab supplies three materially distinct compositions and interactions per stage (15 total), connected as three playable journeys. Use verified public exemplar sources, clearly fictional personal data, simulated Jarvis responses, in-memory state. No private files, paid model calls, account setup, real purchases or production writes. Rules and weights are inspectable prototype hypotheses. Plan changes require confirmation. XP is separate from demonstrated capability and measurement.

## Brand Commitments

Jarvis; clear Chinese task language. Draw on Apace's semantic tokens, CJK typography, accessible controls and quality practice; do not inherit its reading-only layout or ban on gamification.

## Evidence on Hand

NHS Gary running self-report; World Athletics Kipchoge Berlin 2022 2:01:09; Karpathy micrograd source. No actual personal baseline has been provided; all personal values are fictional fixtures. Prior confirmed decisions are in the main worktree .scratch/jarvis/spec.md.

## Product Principles

- Explain why each quest matters for a selected gap.
- Preserve agency through draft/confirm and reversible exploration.
- Recognize action without pretending it proves mastery.
- Make recovery and returning after interruption legitimate choices.
- Let the user compare variants at the same state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Desktop and mobile; keyboard access, labeled fields, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, no color-only state.

## Accepted refinement, 2026-09-30

User prefers C's evidence workshop plus A's explicit capability map and quantified gaps. The new entry is a single shared daily workspace: editable calendar, contextual conversation, visible proposals, progress evidence and state-dependent next actions. Original 15 views remain at variants.html for comparison. This revision is a bounded local simulation, not a claim of Agent Native runtime or model integration.
