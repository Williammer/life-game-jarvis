- A 远征图谱: map topology is the central object. Horizontal branching capability route, a route itinerary for planning, chapter task sheet, camp/checkpoint recovery, newly accessible path at settlement. Pale sky, deep sea blue, ochre trail. Real semantic links, not illustrative scenery.
- B 策略牌桌: opportunity-cost comparison and an assembled quest hand. Gap-priority matrix, visible time/energy reserve, selectable task cards with finite slots, event substitution, actual-vs-planned ledger. Plum table, soft lilac surfaces, restrained brass selection. Cards earn their presence as manipulable tasks.
- C 证据工坊: evidence, experiments and traceable mastery. Exemplar/source dossier, proof-oriented morning brief, acceptance contract, resumable work log, portfolio/stamp report. Pale slate, ink blue, emerald validated evidence. Asymmetric document/editor composition, not a card grid.

All fifteen share one deterministic simulation and truthful inputs. Shared chrome and low-level controls only; each family's composition and primary affordance differ. All five stages have three variants, query-parameter-addressable and switchable without losing state. Variant switcher is unmistakably prototype chrome.
